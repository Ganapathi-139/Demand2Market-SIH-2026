# Future Scope

Demand2Market can be expanded significantly beyond the current implementation.

## Weather Integration

Weather information can be incorporated into demand forecasting.

## Price Intelligence

Market prices can become an additional forecasting factor.

## Inventory Integration

Vendor inventory can be connected to automatically calculate supply-demand gaps.

## Supply Tracking

Incoming supply can be incorporated into shortage and surplus analysis.

## Advanced Forecasting

Machine-learning forecasting models can be introduced alongside the current demand analysis.

## SMS / OTP Authentication

Production phone authentication can be connected to an SMS provider.

## Multi-Market Expansion

The platform can support multiple regions and markets.

## Farmer / Supplier Integration

Future versions can connect vendors directly with suppliers or farmers.

## Automated Alerts

Vendors could receive notifications when a significant shortage or surplus is predicted.

## Analytics

Long-term market analytics can help identify seasonal patterns and changing customer preferences.
