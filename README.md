# Demand2Market

### AI-Powered Demand Intelligence and Procurement Support for Local Markets

Demand2Market is a mobile-first platform that connects customer demand signals with local vendors and converts those signals into actionable market intelligence.

The platform helps vendors understand expected demand, identify potential shortages or surpluses, and make better procurement decisions.

---

## 🎯 Problem

Local vendors often make procurement decisions based on experience, intuition, and incomplete information.

This can result in:

- Overstocking
- Product wastage
- Shortages
- Unpredictable demand
- Inefficient procurement
- Difficulty understanding changing customer requirements

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Demand2Market creates a continuous demand-data flow between customers and vendors.

Customers provide their expected requirements, the platform aggregates demand at the market level, and vendors receive meaningful demand insights and recommendations.

### Core Flow

Customer Demand  
↓  
Market-Level Data Collection  
↓  
Demand Aggregation  
↓  
Historical + Current Analysis  
↓  
AI-Based Insights  
↓  
Demand Forecast  
↓  
Vendor Recommendation  
↓  
Better Procurement Decisions

## 🤖 AI Integration

Demand2Market uses Generative AI to transform collected market information into useful vendor insights.

AI is used for:

- Quality feedback analysis
- Customer sentiment analysis
- Common problem identification
- Product quality recommendations
- Demand-related recommendations

The AI integration is handled through secure server-side Edge Functions.

## 🔐 Security

The application uses PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) to restrict access to user-specific and market-specific data.

Sensitive AI and service-role credentials are kept server-side and are never exposed to the frontend.

---

## 📱 Platform

Demand2Market is designed as a mobile-first Progressive Web App.

The interface is optimized for customers and vendors using smartphones.
