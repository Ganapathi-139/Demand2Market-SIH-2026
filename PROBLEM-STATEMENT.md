# Problem Statement

## The Problem

Local vendors frequently need to decide what and how much to procure without having accurate visibility into upcoming customer demand.

Traditional procurement decisions often depend on:

- Previous experience
- Manual estimation
- Limited customer information
- Unstructured market observations

This creates two major problems.

### Overstock

If vendors procure more products than required, perishable goods can remain unsold and eventually become waste.

### Shortage

If vendors procure less than actual demand, customers may not find the products they need.

---

## The Core Challenge

There is a lack of a simple digital system that can:

1. Collect customer demand before purchase.
2. Aggregate demand at the local market level.
3. Analyze historical and current demand.
4. Provide vendors with understandable forecasts.
5. Support better procurement decisions.

---

## Our Objective

Demand2Market aims to convert customer demand signals into useful information for vendors.

Instead of asking:

> "What should I procure?"

vendors can use the platform to understand:

> "What is the market likely to need?"
