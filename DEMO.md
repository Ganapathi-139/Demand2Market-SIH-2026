## Demo Flow

### Customer

1. Login
2. Select Market
3. View available products
4. Enter expected demand
5. Submit demand
6. View My Demand
7. Submit quality feedback
8. Scan purchase QR
9. Receive reward
10. View streak

---

### Vendor

1. Login
2. Select Market
3. Add products
4. Open dashboard
5. View incoming demand
6. View 7-day analytics
7. View 30-day analytics
8. View tomorrow's demand
9. View procurement recommendation
10. View quality insights

---

## Recommended Demonstration

For the live demonstration:

**Browser 1:** Customer

**Browser 2 / Incognito:** Vendor

Submit demand from the customer side and immediately show the corresponding vendor-side update.

Then demonstrate:

Customer → Quality Feedback → Vendor AI Insight

Finally demonstrate:

Customer → QR Verification → Token + Streak
