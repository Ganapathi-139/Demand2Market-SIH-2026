# Solution

Demand2Market creates a digital connection between customers and vendors within a local market.

Customers submit what they expect to purchase.

The system stores and aggregates these demand signals.

Vendors can then view:

- Demand trends
- Tomorrow's expected demand
- Historical demand
- Procurement recommendations
- Quality insights

---

## How It Works

### Step 1 — Customer

The customer selects a market and chooses products available from vendors in that market.

### Step 2 — Demand Entry

The customer enters the expected quantity.

The system stores the canonical quantity in grams.

### Step 3 — Market Aggregation

Demand records are associated with the selected market and product.

### Step 4 — Analysis

The platform analyzes historical and upcoming demand.

### Step 5 — Vendor Dashboard

Vendors see demand through charts, forecasts, and recommendations.

### Step 6 — Continuous Feedback

Customers can submit product quality feedback and verify purchases through QR codes.

This creates additional data for improving market intelligence.

---

## Result

The platform converts:

Customer Intent → Market Data → Intelligence → Vendor Action
