# Features

## Customer Features

### Market Selection
Customers select the local market they want to purchase from.

### Demand Entry
Customers can submit expected demand for available products.

### Quantity Support
Quantities can be entered in grams or kilograms.

### My Demand
Customers can view their previously submitted demand.

### Quality Feedback
Customers can rate products and optionally provide comments.

### QR Verification
Customers can verify purchases using QR codes.

### Rewards
Successful verification can generate tokens.

### Monthly Streak
Consecutive verification days contribute to a monthly streak.

---

## Vendor Features

### Product Management
Vendors can add or remove products they sell.

### Demand Dashboard
Vendors can monitor customer demand for their market.

### 7-Day Analytics
Shows recent demand trends.

### 30-Day Analytics
Provides a longer demand view.

### Tomorrow's Demand
Customer-booked demand for tomorrow is included in the vendor forecast.

### Procurement Recommendation
The system provides an expected procurement range based on demand analysis.

### Quality Insights
AI analyzes customer quality feedback.

### Real-Time Updates
New demand and other relevant updates can appear without manually refreshing the application.

---

## Security Features

- Authentication
- Row Level Security
- Market-specific data access
- Customer-specific data access
